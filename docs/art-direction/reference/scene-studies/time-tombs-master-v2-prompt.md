## Exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create an original art-direction painting for an extraordinary animated 16-bit science-fiction screensaver: the Valley of the Time Tombs from Dan Simmons's Hyperion books, at violet evening twilight. Ultra-wide landscape panorama, about 3:1. Make your own strong composition from this description. There is NO existing Time Tombs scene image to match.
Attached images are authentic Sega Genesis Castlevania Bloodlines and Pitfall screenshots. They are references ONLY for economy of shapes, coherent palette, depth through reduced distant detail, and exceptional game-background craft. Do not reproduce their subjects or layouts. Develop a new alien valley.
The valley must feel immense, ancient, remote and quietly uncanny. The camera should look INTO a deep winding arroyo from near the entrance, not straight across a lineup of monuments. Sculptural canyon walls and a winding pilgrim path lead the eye past overlapping depth planes into a long distance. Place the most distant palace almost a kilometer down-valley, visibly smaller and hazier than nearer structures. Use space, occlusion, atmospheric color and human scale to give each tomb a believable distance. Avoid evenly spaced objects on a single horizontal shelf. Allow genuinely different silhouettes, widths and viewpoints. Give the image a deliberate primary focal point and a small warm secondary focal point.
Canonical subjects, in sequence along the path from near valley entrance to far end (not necessarily left-to-right across the image):
1. The Sphinx: immense heavy stone blocks, intricate stone carving, a low enigmatic curved crown and broad sweeping appendages suggesting outflung wings. The represented form is neither human nor lion and may not be a creature at all. No Egyptian face, no paws required; invent ambiguous monumental alien architecture from this description. Its maze-like passages have a minute recessed doorway.
2. The Jade Tomb: deep green walls become translucent only at twilight, enclosing broad vaults and winding catacombs. Invent an organic mineral architectural vault with a recessed entrance. No obligatory pyramid, no round holes peppered across the exterior, no bright wavy stripes. Subtle green transmitted light belongs inside its walls.
3. The Obelisk: a tall windowless ebony tower enclosing a steep spiral ramp. Restrained massive dark faces, tiny doorway, not a rune-covered fantasy lighthouse. Allow an unfamiliar elegant obelisk silhouette.
4. The centrally placed Crystal Monolith: the largest tomb, its FLAT roof reaches the canyon rim. Its smooth crystal surface has no carved design, doorway or windows. A massive flat-topped volume, not a pointed quartz cluster. Restrained inner refraction and broad violet planes reveal its material.
5. The three Cave Tombs: exactly three tiny shadowed entrances cut into pink canyon stone, identifiable mainly by worn approach paths. These should be subtle landscape details, not a line of huge arches or masonry towers.
6. The Shrike Palace: remote at the end of the valley, black barbed and serrated buttresses, sharp flanges and outflung needle spires reminiscent of thorns. Alien architecture, not a European cathedral.
A small expedition camp near the Sphinx on the foreground valley floor has patched canvas tents in plum and cream, equipment, seven tiny adult pilgrims (one cradles an infant) and a small amber campfire. Their human scale makes the tombs enormous. Keep them secondary, with open walking ground. No other people or cities.
Mood and visual construction: calm, haunted, spacious, dusk desert cold arriving after heat. Deep indigo upper sky, dusty violet atmosphere, pink sandstone canyon, restrained amber last light, cool violet shadows. One consistent low warm key and cool sky fill. Stone has stable large light planes; no glowing outlines around everything. Foreground gets articulated edges, purposeful pixel clusters and legible material detail. Faraway surfaces merge into simpler quieter masses and lose microtexture and contrast. Mineral translucency is localized and subtle.
Medium: beautifully art-directed chunky hand-painted pixel-game concept art, not photorealistic and not a 3D render. Favor composition, material and mass over busy fine texture. It should feel like the ambitious establishing background of an exceptional 1990s console adventure. No HUD, text, labels, logo, watermark, photo collage, single ground rail, side-by-side monument catalog, uniform speckle, or generic fantasy castle.
