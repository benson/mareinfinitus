# Four independent Time Tombs directions

Generated September 5, 2026 with the built-in imagegen tool. Each image used a
separate prompt and no reference images: neither the existing game scene, prior
concepts nor the attached procedural example was supplied to imagegen.
The attachment informed the requested method, not the scene subject.

Four independent 256-character alphanumeric strings were generated in PowerShell
using System.Security.Cryptography.RandomNumberGenerator.GetInt32. The exact
strings, interpretations and final prompts are in directions.json. The shell
procedure is preserved in generate-seeds.ps1; running it creates new random
strings, not the four original strings. Pattern interpretations are subjective
creative associations, not hidden numerical facts or imagegen random seeds.
No seed text appears in the images.

Open index.html for the comparison gallery. Each card links to the original PNG.

## 1. Porcelain Dawn

Image: 01-porcelain-dawn.png

Chalk, celadon and apricot. High overlook, open terraces, first sunlight.

Seed interpretation: The near-opening EE and later TT suggest paired ledges. The embedded 90, then 65, then 58 suggest an elevated view stepping down through terraces. The repeated circular O/o forms interrupt the angular capitals: soft mineral bowls inside a rectilinear terrain rhythm. These are subjective visual associations, not a numerical decoding.

## 2. The Black Sun

Image: 02-the-black-sun.png

Petrol teal and copper. Low basin view, eclipsed midday, monumental silhouettes.

Seed interpretation: The alternating u0h0, the O/x-heavy O...xoX region, and the later U00 suggest interruption, rings and occlusion. The terminal 677 feels like a rising three-beat cadence: one dark mass balanced by two distant light accents. This becomes an eclipse and a concentric basin rather than a decorative motif.

## 3. Blue Hour Labyrinth

Image: 03-blue-hour-labyrinth.png

Cobalt and silver. Sheltered canyon, cold predawn, layered switchbacks.

Seed interpretation: Paired ii, 11, aa, JJ and vv appear across the string like repeated folds with changing orientation. The ending 05C evokes cold, low-temperature light. Descending and returning V/v forms suggest a switchback route seen through successive rock screens.

## 4. Saffron Tempest

Image: 04-saffron-tempest.png

Saffron, rust and aubergine. Low diagonal view, late-afternoon dust, wind and shelter.

Seed interpretation: zZ, repeated W/w/X forms and the central 22tt suggest crossed diagonals, alternating wind pressure and parallel struts. The early 12 and later 95 form a steep numeric jump: a low viewpoint against a towering diagonal wall. The late FF and ww motifs suggest two bands of force, used as dust strata rather than literal symbols.


These are four choices for art direction, not accepted native-pixel assets.
They deliberately vary time, palette, camera, terrain structure and mood. The
live scene and production code were not changed. Once a direction is selected,
refine the canonical object designs and human-scale detail before translating
into tiles, maps and independent animation layers. In particular, several
Palace silhouettes still read as conventional castles, and generated masonry,
cave openings and tiny people need a deliberate scale pass.
